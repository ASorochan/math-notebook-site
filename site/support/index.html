Support

Answers for common questions and a direct path to report reproducible problems.

System requirements

Math Notebook requires macOS 13 Ventura or later and runs natively on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Frequently asked questions

Does Math Notebook require an internet connection?

No. Calculations, symbolic operations, plots, document storage, and exports run locally on your Mac.

Where are my notebooks stored?

You choose the location when saving a .mathnote document. Math Notebook does not upload your documents to a developer-operated service.

Can I export my work?

Yes. Use the app’s Export command to create a PDF, or Print to use the standard macOS print workflow.

Why does a symbolic operation return a limited result?

Symbolic coverage is intentionally bounded. Try simplifying the expression, splitting it into smaller steps, or using a numerical plot when a closed symbolic form is not supported.

How should I report a problem?

Include your macOS version, the Math Notebook version, the expression or steps that reproduce the issue, and what you expected to happen. Remove personal information before attaching a notebook or screenshot.

Report an issue

Email restive_drizzly_89@icloud.com for private support questions. For reproducible bugs, you can also use GitHub Issues. Issues are public, so do not include personal, confidential, or sensitive information.
