Privacy Policy

Math Notebook is designed to work entirely on your Mac. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

In short: no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no cloud service.

Data stored on your Mac

Math Notebook stores documents only where you choose to save them. The app may also store local preferences, such as text size and whether the getting-started guide has been shown. This information remains on your Mac and is not sent to the developer.

Network access and third parties

Math Notebook does not require an account or internet connection. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party software development kits that collect data.

Export and sharing

When you export, print, move, or share a notebook or PDF, macOS performs the action you request. You control the destination and recipients.

Children’s privacy

Because Math Notebook does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes the app’s data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before that version is released. The effective date at the top of this page will also change.
